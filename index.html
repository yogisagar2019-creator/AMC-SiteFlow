🏢 About AMC

Company profile, work experience and contact information.

Introduction

Anil Mehta Co. (AMC) is an established civil construction and infrastructure organization with more than 30 years of experience in the construction field. The company has developed extensive experience in civil engineering, infrastructure development, project execution, and site management, with a focus on systematic planning, quality work, efficient resource utilization, and timely project execution.

Work & Project Experience

Road Construction & Development

AMC has experience in road construction, development, and associated civil infrastructure works. Projects are carried out with proper planning, site coordination, material management, workforce management, and attention to construction quality.

Bridge & Related Civil Works

The company undertakes bridge construction and related civil works, including supporting structural and site-development activities. Proper coordination of manpower, materials, machinery, and site activities is maintained throughout project execution.

Railway Civil Works

AMC also works on civil infrastructure associated with railway projects. These works involve coordinated site execution, construction activities, material management, and implementation according to project-specific requirements.

Railway Underpasses & Culverts

Experience also includes railway underpasses, culverts, drainage structures, and other related civil infrastructure works. Such projects involve activities such as excavation, concrete work, structural development, and coordinated site execution.

Government Infrastructure Projects

AMC undertakes infrastructure projects according to defined project requirements and specifications. Site supervision, workforce coordination, material management, quality practices, and project documentation form an important part of the execution process.

Professional Work Approach

AMC follows a systematic approach to construction and project execution, focusing on proper planning, site coordination, material and machinery management, workforce utilization, quality control, and organized project execution. The company aims to maintain efficient coordination between different site activities while meeting project requirements.
